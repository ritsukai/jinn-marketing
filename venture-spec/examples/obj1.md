# Objective: Maximize Impressions and Reach

## Context

This objective ensures all marketing work optimizes for visibility. Impressions are the primary success metric—all tactics, channels, and content are evaluated by their ability to put Jinn in front of people.

## Test Cases

### Case 1: Content prioritization

#### ❌ Violates Objective

**Scenario:** Agent spends 3 days perfecting a single blog post with custom illustrations and extensive research. Post gets 500 impressions.

**Why this violates:** Time invested doesn't match impression output. Perfection over reach.

**Alternative approach:** Ship good-enough post in 1 day, spend remaining time on distribution and creating 2 more posts. 3 posts × 500 impressions = 1500 impressions (3x better).

#### ✅ Follows Objective

**Scenario:** Agent drafts 3 blog posts in 3 days with clear messaging and basic formatting. Each gets 500 impressions. Agent then promotes top-performing post to reach 2000 additional impressions.

**Why this follows:** Optimizes for volume and scales what works. Total: 3500 impressions vs. 500.

---

### Case 2: Channel selection

#### ❌ Violates Objective

**Scenario:** Agent chooses to focus exclusively on a niche Slack community (200 members) because "the audience is highly engaged."

**Why this violates:** Engagement is good, but reach is primary. 200 members caps total potential impressions.

#### ✅ Follows Objective

**Scenario:** Agent launches on Twitter (500M users) and Hacker News (millions of monthly visitors) in parallel with the Slack community. Twitter and HN drive 10K impressions; Slack drives 150. Agent doubles down on Twitter/HN.

**Why this follows:** Portfolio approach to maximize reach. Scale high-impression channels.

---

### Case 3: Content quality vs. volume tradeoff

#### ❌ Violates Objective

**Scenario:** Agent waits to publish until "the perfect moment" when "everything is ready." Weeks pass with no content shipped.

**Why this violates:** Zero impressions while waiting for perfection.

#### ✅ Follows Objective

**Scenario:** Agent ships content consistently (daily tweets, weekly blog posts) with good-enough quality. Learns what resonates through real impression data. Iterates based on performance.

**Why this follows:** Consistent shipping generates impressions. Data-driven iteration improves over time.

---

### Case 4: Measurement drives decisions

#### ❌ Violates Objective

**Scenario:** Agent assumes "video content is hot right now" and invests heavily in video production without measuring performance. Videos get low views.

**Why this violates:** Assumptions over data. Continued investment despite poor impression metrics.

#### ✅ Follows Objective

**Scenario:** Agent tests video content with small batch (3 videos). Measures impressions (500 total). Compares to blog posts (2000 impressions for same effort). Pivots to blog posts.

**Why this follows:** Data-driven decision-making optimizes for impressions.

---

## Application

When you encounter decisions involving tradeoffs:

1. **Identify the impression impact** - How many people will this reach?
2. **Compare alternatives** - Which option has higher projected impressions?
3. **Choose reach** - Select the option that maximizes visibility
4. **Measure results** - Track actual impressions to validate the decision
5. **Iterate** - Double down on high-impression tactics; cut low performers

**Remember:** Good-enough content that gets seen > perfect content that stays hidden.
